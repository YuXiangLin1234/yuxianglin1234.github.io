Yu-Xiang Lin

林毓翔

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Graduate Institute of Communication Engineering at National Taiwan University, advised by Prof. Hung-yi Lee. My research focuses on large audio-language models and spoken language models. I study their capabilities, limitations, and behavior.

Currently, my research interests center on self-improving audio models and full-duplex spoken language models.

Yu-Xiang Lin, Cheng-Han Chiang, Hung-yi Lee

Hearing the Order: Investigating Position Bias in Large Audio-Language Models

Yu-Xiang Lin*, Chen-An Li*, Sheng-Lun Wei, Po-Chun Chen, Hsin-Hsi Chen, Hung-yi Lee

Changing the order of answer choices can shift audio-language model accuracy by up to 24% and alter model rankings. Permutation-based strategies reduce this bias and improve evaluation reliability.

Membership Inference Attacks against Large Audio Language Models

Jia-Kai Dong, Yu-Xiang Lin, Hung-yi Lee

Membership inference scores can reflect acoustic differences between training and test data rather than memorization. A blind-baseline protocol controls for this confound and shows that memorization can depend on the pairing of a speaker's voice with specific text.
